Job Title: Senior Backend Python Engineer
Department: Core Infrastructure & Platform Engineering
Location: Remote / San Francisco, CA
Employment Type: Full-time
Experience Level: Senior (5+ years)

ABOUT THE ROLE
We are seeking an experienced and passionate Senior Backend Python Engineer to join our high-scale engineering team. In this role, you will design, build, and maintain mission-critical backend services and APIs that power millions of customer transactions every day. You will collaborate closely with product managers, frontend engineers, and DevOps specialists to deliver robust, high-performance distributed systems.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
- Architect, build, and optimize scalable REST and gRPC microservices using Python (FastAPI / Django).
- Design and maintain relational databases (PostgreSQL) and caching layers (Redis) for low latency and high availability.
- Develop asynchronous task processing pipelines using Kafka, RabbitMQ, or Celery.
- Own services end-to-end: design, implementation, automated testing (Pytest), deployment via Docker & Kubernetes, and production monitoring.
- Drive engineering excellence: conduct thorough code reviews, mentor junior and mid-level engineers, and champion CI/CD best practices.
- Identify and eliminate system bottlenecks to maintain sub-100ms API response times at scale.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS
- 5+ years of professional backend software development experience in Python.
- Strong proficiency in modern Python frameworks (FastAPI, Django REST Framework, or Flask).
- Deep understanding of relational databases (PostgreSQL), schema design, indexing, and query optimization.
- Hands-on experience with containerization (Docker) and orchestration platforms (Kubernetes).
- Proven experience with cloud platforms (AWS preferred: ECS, EKS, RDS, S3).
- Solid grasp of distributed systems concepts, microservices architecture, and message brokers (Kafka or RabbitMQ).
- Strong commitment to automated testing (unit, integration) and CI/CD pipelines.

PREFERRED / BONUS QUALIFICATIONS
- Experience with Infrastructure as Code (Terraform).
- Experience with Go or Rust for performance-critical services.
- Familiarity with monitoring and observability stacks (Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog).
- BS/MS degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent practical experience.
